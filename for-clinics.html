What you get

The operating layer, part by part.

Six things every clinic runs anyway, kept on one record instead of six systems that do not talk.

E-prescribing

Your prescriber writes into a structured record: patient, preparation and strength captured up front. No PDF, no re-keying at the other end.

Pharmacy routing

Routed only to a licensed 503A partner that holds the right state licensure and carries the preparation.

Settlement

Your card on file is charged when the prescriber signs. The pharmacy is the merchant of record; the Pharity fee is a separate line.

Order tracking

One queue for every order: drafted, awaiting signature, filling, in transit, delivered. Nobody chases status by email.

Exclusion & license screening

Every prescriber is screened against federal exclusion lists and license status at onboarding, then again on a schedule.

Audit trail

Every state transition is timestamped and attributable to a person, and the whole trail exports.

How a prescription moves

From intake to delivery, on one record.

Nothing routes until a licensed prescriber signs. Every step after that writes to the same record.

  1. 01

    Intake

    Patient form, ID and consent land on the clinical record.

  2. 02

    A prescriber signs

    Yours, or an optional white-label telehealth layer. Many clinics run both.

  3. 03

    The card is charged

    Charged at signature. The Pharity fee is its own line.

  4. 04

    The pharmacy fills

    A licensed 503A partner in that state compounds and dispenses it.

  5. 05

    Tracked to the patient

    Tracking posts back to the same order. Status is never a phone call.
