For Brands

Add a prescription-grade program without becoming a medical practice.

Pharity provides the technology and the pharmacy routing behind cash-pay compounded therapy programs. Licensed clinicians practicing within independently owned and operated professional practices handle all clinical care, including evaluation and prescribing. Licensed 503A and 503B compounding pharmacies handle all dispensing. You own your brand, your members, and your member experience.

How it works

01

You market the program

To your members and to the public, in your own brand and at your own price.

02

A member completes a clinical intake

On your branded portal, with a licensed clinician.

03

The clinician independently decides

Whether to prescribe, and what. That decision is theirs alone.

04

We route it, the pharmacy ships it

To a licensed compounding pharmacy holding a licence in your member's state, direct to their door.

Pricing

One onboarding fee, then a flat monthly.

Tiered on active member seats, never on what gets prescribed. No per-prescription and no per-consult fees, ever.

The onboarding fee covers the build: your branded portal, the integration, and launch support. It is scoped on a call, so we quote after the conversation.

Pharity Health LLC does not provide medical or pharmacy services, does not employ or contract with prescribing clinicians, and does not influence clinical decisions. All professional medical services are provided by licensed clinicians practicing within independently owned and operated professional practices. All medications are dispensed by licensed compounding pharmacies.

Payment does not guarantee the prescribing or dispensing of any medication. A valid, patient-specific prescription from a licensed clinician is required.

Compounded preparations are not FDA-approved and have not been reviewed by FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

Programs are cash-pay only and are not billed to any insurance plan or government health care program.

One coordinated layer between wellness clinics and licensed 503A compounding pharmacies.
