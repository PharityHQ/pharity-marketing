For pharmacies

Built on the right side of compliance

Partnering with Pharity brings end-to-end peace of mind in a fragmented landscape.

The model is intentionally clear about who provides care, who fills prescriptions and how Pharity coordinates the handoffs.

  • Not a pharmacy or prescriber

    Licensed prescribers retain clinical responsibility. Licensed 503A partner pharmacies fill under their own licenses.

  • Clean business workflows

    Built for B2B wellness operations, with responsibilities stated clearly across prescribers, pharmacies and Pharity.

  • Payment routing clarity

    The pharmacy is the merchant of record. The platform fee settles on its own line, never folded into the price of the medication.

  • Sensitive data handled carefully

    Patient identifiers and clinical context reach only the partner licensed to act on them.

  • HIPAA-aligned infrastructure

    Protected health information stays inside the systems and partner workflows that need it. A BAA is available on request.

  • Pharmacy and clinic coordination

    Pharity coordinates the handoffs between the clinic and the partner pharmacy, and keeps the record.

Prescriber-directed. Pharity does not prescribe, dispense, or practice medicine, and does not provide medical advice.
