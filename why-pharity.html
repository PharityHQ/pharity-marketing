What stays with whom

Every part is owned by whoever is licensed to own it.

Pharity coordinates the handoffs and keeps the record. It does not take over any part of the work that belongs to a license.

The prescriber

Writes, and keeps clinical responsibility. Nothing routes and nothing is charged until a licensed prescriber signs.

The pharmacy

Compounds and dispenses under its own license, and is the merchant of record on the medication.

Pharity

Coordinates intake, routing, settlement and the audit trail. Not a pharmacy, a prescriber or a dispenser.

What runs underneath

The record is written as it happens.

Screening runs before an order can move, and every transition is timestamped as it occurs. Nothing is reconstructed from email afterwards.

Apply for access

  • Screening runs first

    Prescribers are screened against exclusion lists and license status at onboarding, then re-checked on a schedule. Routing respects each pharmacy's licensure, state by state.

  • A trail written as it happens

    Every state change on the record above carries a time and an actor, written at the moment it occurs. The trail exports whole.

  • Cash-pay only

    Pharity does not bill or accept insurance, Medicare, Medicaid, or Tricare. Settlement runs against the clinic's card on file and nothing else.
