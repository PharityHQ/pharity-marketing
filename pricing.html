Optional layer

A white-label telehealth layer, under your own brand.

There are two reasons to add it, and they often apply together. Some clinics have no prescriber on staff and need a prescribing route. Others already have prescribers and want to take the practice online, seeing patients beyond the ones who walk in. Keeping your own prescribers and running this alongside them is a normal setup, not a choice between the two.

Patients see your brand through booking and intake, and that is where the white-labelling stops. The prescribers are independent: they are not employed by your clinic, and they take clinical direction from neither your clinic nor from us. Each keeps clinical responsibility for every decision they make, including the decision not to prescribe. Pharity does not prescribe, dispense, or practice medicine.

This layer is rolling out as provider partners are onboarded. Ask on your call for current availability in your state.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is there a setup fee?
Not for base access. Clinics connect at $0 and partner pharmacies join at $0. The marketing plans and Pharity Approved are month to month, the telehealth layer will be on the same terms once it opens, and a launch build is quoted after a scoping call.
Are the monthly layers the only cost?
The layers on this page are the subscription side, and they are the only recurring charges. Separately, what Pharity charges on an order sits as its own line on the record beside what the pharmacy charges, so the two are never folded together. That rate card is set against the volume you actually run, and you see it in full before anything is signed.
How does the optional telehealth layer work?
There are two reasons to add it. Some clinics have no prescriber on staff and need a prescribing route. Others have prescribers already and want to see patients online rather than only in the room. You can keep your own prescribers and run this alongside them, and it carries your brand rather than ours. The branding is the patient-facing experience only. The prescribers are independent, are not employed by your clinic, and take clinical direction from neither your clinic nor from us. They retain clinical responsibility for what they write, including when the answer is not to prescribe. Pharity does not prescribe, dispense, or practice medicine. The layer is rolling out as provider partners are onboarded, so ask on your call for current availability.
Can a pharmacy pay for preferred routing?
No. Routing is matched on state licensure, catalog coverage and capacity, and nothing a pharmacy pays changes where a prescription goes. Pharity Approved is a marketing layer rather than a routing one: it puts the Pharity Approved badge on your partner profile and gives your listing a featured position in the catalog clinics browse. It changes what a clinic sees, not where an order is sent, and it is not a second layer of vetting. Every partner pharmacy's state licensure is checked on every route whether it carries the badge or not.
What does Pharity Approved include?
The Pharity Approved badge on your partner profile, a featured position in the partner catalog clinics browse, an expanded profile covering capabilities and turnaround, and inclusion in Pharity campaigns and partner announcements. The badge is tied to your licensure staying current and is reviewed each year. It is month to month, and it is priced on a call against your footprint.
When is the clinic charged?
The card on file is charged when the prescriber signs. What the pharmacy charges for the preparation and what Pharity charges appear as separate lines on the same record, so the two are never bundled together.
Do you bill insurance?
No. Pharity is cash-pay only and does not bill or accept insurance, Medicare, Medicaid, or Tricare.
Can I cancel?
Yes. The marketing plans and Pharity Approved are month to month, and the telehealth layer will be on the same terms once it opens. Base access for clinics and for pharmacies carries no commitment at all.
